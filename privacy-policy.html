Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ZelvoraLead ("we", "us", "our") provides lead-generation and customer-acquisition systems for service businesses in South Africa. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit the "Get My Free Lead Audit" form, we collect the details you provide directly: your name, business name, phone/WhatsApp number, website (if given), industry, and what you're looking to get more of. We do not ask for payment information on this site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the free lead audit you requested
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email about our services
  • To understand which pages and channels bring visitors to this site (see Analytics below)

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

WhatsApp & Communication

If you contact us via WhatsApp or provide a WhatsApp number, we'll use it to follow up on your enquiry. Standard messaging rates from your provider may apply.

Analytics

This site may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns (e.g. which pages are visited, general location, device type). This data is not used to personally identify you individually where it can reasonably be avoided.

Cookies

This site may use essential cookies required for the site to function, and analytics cookies to measure traffic. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and maintain business records, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your Rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information held by us. To make a request, contact us via WhatsApp at the number listed on this site.

Third-Party Links

Our "Example Builds" section links to websites we've built for other, separate businesses. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us via WhatsApp through the contact options on our homepage.
