Questions

FAQ

What exactly is a ZelvoraLead Machine?

A connected system — traffic, website, AI response, qualification, follow-up and measurement — built to turn attention into enquiries and enquiries into customers, instead of running each piece separately.

Do you build the website too?

Yes — as the conversion front end of the machine, not as a standalone product. A site with no acquisition system behind it is just a brochure.

Can you run Google Ads?

Yes. Ad spend and our management fee are kept separate and transparent, so you always know exactly where every rand went.

Can the system work with my existing website?

Often, yes — response, qualification, follow-up and tracking can connect to a site you already have. If the site itself is the bottleneck, we'll tell you honestly rather than bolt automation onto something that doesn't convert.

Can you connect WhatsApp?

Yes — for South African customers it's often the fastest path from search to conversation, so it's built in as a first-class channel, not an afterthought.

What can the AI agent actually do?

Answer common questions, capture enquiries, ask qualifying questions, collect contact details, recommend a service, and book appointments — then hand the conversation to you when it actually needs a person.

Do I need to run paid advertising?

No. Paid traffic is one channel that can feed the machine, not a requirement — organic search, Google Business Profile and referrals can too.

How do I know if the system is working?

We report on calls, enquiries, bookings and cost per lead — measurable actions, not impressions or reach.

Which businesses are a good fit?

Service businesses where customers search locally, one job is worth real money, and speed of response matters — see "Who We Work With" above.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what's actually needed — a new website, ad management, AI response, or all three connected. We'll give you a real number after the free Lead Audit, not a generic price on a page.

Do you have client case studies?

Not yet published — we're not going to invent testimonials or results to look otherwise. What you can see right now is real work: the example builds linked above. Case studies with real campaign numbers will follow as client campaigns run, with full visibility from day one for anyone who comes on board early.
